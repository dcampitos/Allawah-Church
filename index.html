New here?

New to the area, looking for a church, or never been before — you're welcome here. Here's what to expect.

What happens at a service?

Worship band, prayer, giving, and a Bible-based message for everyday life. About 75 minutes.

When should I arrive?

Our Sunday service starts at 10:30am. Aim to arrive about 15 minutes early (around 10:15am) to park, grab a coffee and find a seat.

What should I wear?

Very casual — whatever you're comfortable in.

Anything for my kids?

Yes. Kids Church runs every Sunday with safe, age-appropriate programs.

Will I be singled out?

Never. No standing, waving or being put on the spot.

Where is it?

What we believe

The short version. We're a Bible-believing church, and we'd love to talk through any of it with you.

God

One God — Father, Son and Spirit — creator and redeemer, worthy of all praise.

The Bible

God's inspired, trustworthy word, sufficient for knowing Him and living for Him.

Jesus

Fully God and fully human; He died for our sin, rose again and will return.

Salvation

A relationship with God comes only through Jesus, entirely by grace.

The Holy Spirit

Lives in every believer, keeps Jesus central and equips us to serve.

The Church

A community gathered around Christ and His word, bearing witness to Him.
